About Us

Shuhari Finance Private Limited, is a non-deposit taking Non-Banking Financial Company. We believe that fair, transparent access to credit should be available to every individual.

Who we are

Accessible finance, done responsibly

Shuhari Finance is the licensed lending arm of NIRA, a digital lending platform serving India’s working population. As an RBI-regulated NBFC, we lend from our own book under a clear framework of fair practices, transparent pricing and strong customer protection.

Our approach combines a digital-first borrower experience with the discipline and accountability expected of a regulated financial institution.

Our vision

To be a trusted, technology-led lender that makes relevant credit available to every creditworthy Indian — fairly, transparently and responsibly.

Our mission

To bring relevant and accessible financial products to India’s mass market, building long-term trust through transparency, fair dealing and customer-first service.
