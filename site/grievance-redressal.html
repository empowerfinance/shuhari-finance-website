Level 4 · Reserve Bank of India

RBI Ombudsman & Sachet portal

If your complaint is not resolved within days, or you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may escalate to the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS):

  • RBI Integrated Ombudsman / Complaint Management System (CMS): cms.rbi.org.in · toll-free
  • RBI Sachet portal (complaints against NBFCs): sachet.rbi.org.in

Our full grievance redressal process is set out in our Grievance Redressal Policy (PDF).
